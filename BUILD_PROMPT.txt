======================================================================
FIRST PRINCIPLE — WHAT VOWMARK IS
======================================================================

VOWMARK is a public commitment bond protocol.

An issuer makes one externally verifiable public promise, freezes how that promise should be evidenced, chooses a non-issuer remedy address, sets a maturity deadline and final review deadline, and locks GEN behind the promise.

After maturity, any wallet may trigger review. GenLayer validators independently retrieve only the frozen public evidence anchors and answer the bounded semantic question:

“Under the commitment and verification rule exactly as recorded, and considering only the frozen admissible public evidence, does the evidence establish that the commitment was fulfilled by the stated maturity deadline?”

The product outcomes are:
- FULFILLED
- BREACHED
- INCONCLUSIVE

If no reliable conclusive answer exists by the final review deadline, the terminal expiry result is:
- EXPIRED_UNRESOLVED

Economic consequence:
- FULFILLED -> issuer receives credit for the locked bond
- BREACHED -> immutable remedy address receives credit for the locked bond
- INCONCLUSIVE -> no credit yet; bond remains locked and review may retry when legal
- EXPIRED_UNRESOLVED -> issuer receives credit for the bond, while the unresolved outcome remains permanently visible

The LLM/validator NEVER chooses a wei amount or payout address.

- normalized URL
- host/origin
- fetch status class
- bounded content length
- content-window hash/digest
- aggregate source-set digest

Do not include inherently non-reproducible per-validator local timestamps in the consensus-critical digest.

======================================================================
TEMPORAL JUDGMENT
======================================================================

VOWMARK judges whether a promise was fulfilled BY THE MATURITY DEADLINE.

A page existing at review time is not automatically proof that the obligation was completed in time.

Validators should prefer durable time-bearing evidence such as:
- commit/tag/release metadata
- on-chain block/transaction state
- dated public report metadata
- other source-backed timestamps

If timing cannot be reliably established, return INCONCLUSIVE.

A source outage is not automatically BREACHED.

BREACHED requires accessible evidence that materially establishes non-fulfillment, lateness, contradiction or another clear failure under the frozen verification rule.

======================================================================
CONSENSUS-CRITICAL JUDGMENT
======================================================================

Keep model output small and machine-checkable.

The consensus-critical result must include at minimum:
- stable commitment/review binding
- evidence snapshot/source-set digest or equivalent material binding
- verdict enum: FULFILLED | BREACHED | INCONCLUSIVE

A concise explanation/source assessment may be stored for UX/provenance, but free-form wording must not be the primary field that consensus depends on.

Reject malformed/unknown outputs.
Never default malformed output to FULFILLED or BREACHED.

If leader/validators cannot reproduce materially compatible evidence/result, allow protocol consensus to fail rather than fabricating certainty.

Protocol UNDETERMINED is NOT the same as product INCONCLUSIVE.

======================================================================
PRODUCT OUTCOMES
======================================================================

FULFILLED
- evidence materially establishes the commitment was satisfied by maturity
- conclusive
- no further review
- bond resolves to issuer
- permanent issuer record increments fulfilled

BREACHED
- evidence materially establishes the commitment was not satisfied by maturity
- conclusive
- no further review
- bond resolves to immutable remedy address
- permanent issuer record increments breached

INCONCLUSIVE
- evidence cannot reliably support either conclusive outcome
- nonterminal before final review deadline
- bond remains locked
- attempt is append-only
- retry allowed only after cooldown and while within review window
- permanent history preserves the attempt

EXPIRED_UNRESOLVED
- no conclusive verdict by final review deadline
- terminal
- bond resolves back to issuer under V1 policy
- permanent issuer record increments unresolved
- UI must not describe this as success

======================================================================
REVIEW / RETRY / REPLAY RULES
======================================================================

Any wallet may trigger review after maturity and before the final review deadline.

The issuer does not get exclusive review control.
The remedy address does not get exclusive review control.

Enforce:
- no review before maturity
- no review after conclusive result
- no review after terminal expiry
- nonzero retry cooldown
- bounded attempt history if required for storage safety
- append-only attempt records
- exact duplicate/same snapshot must not create unbounded duplicate state
- terminal settlement is idempotent
- repeated clicks do not double-resolve the bond

If a transaction is submitted and the browser refreshes, recover from the known hash and canonical readback. Never blindly resubmit because a poll timed out.

ACCOUNTING INVARIANTS
======================================================================

The contract layer must prove:
- a commitment locks exactly one declared bond
- bond amount is immutable
- model never chooses amount
- model never chooses recipient
- issuer/remedy recipient fixed at issuance
